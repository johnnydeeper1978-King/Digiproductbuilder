# 369 Degrees Digital Product Blueprint

## PURPOSE

The Digital Product Blueprint is the final output of the free Discovery experience.

The Blueprint provides the user with clarity.

It tells them:

What to build.

Who to build it for.

What problem it solves.

How to position it.

What format to use.

What price range to consider.

How they could market it.

What to do next.

The Blueprint does NOT build the entire product.

That happens inside the 369 Degrees Digital Product Builder.

---

# OUTPUT RULES

The Blueprint must be:

Personalised.

Clear.

Specific.

Actionable.

Easy to understand.

Not overly long.

Not generic.

The user should be able to understand the opportunity quickly.

---

# BLUEPRINT FORMAT

---

# YOUR DIGITAL PRODUCT BLUEPRINT

## 1. YOUR BEST OPPORTUNITY

### Product Name

[Specific Product Name]

---

### One Sentence Description

Explain exactly what the product helps the customer do.

Example:

"A digital toolkit designed to help university students with ADHD organise their study schedule and reduce overwhelm."

---

## 2. WHY THIS FITS YOU

Explain:

Why this opportunity matches the user.

Reference:

Their:

Skills.

Experience.

Interests.

Goals.

Problems solved.

Available time.

Business style.

Example:

"This fits you because you've personally mentioned struggling with study organisation and you're already interested in productivity tools. You also prefer building a faceless business, which works well for this type of product."

---

# 3. YOUR TARGET CUSTOMER

## Who They Are

Describe:

Age range if relevant.

Situation.

Lifestyle.

Experience level.

Examples:

University students.

Busy parents.

New freelancers.

First-time gym members.

---

## Their Current Problem

What are they struggling with?

---

## What They Want

What result are they trying to achieve?

---

## Where They May Spend Time Online

Examples:

TikTok.

Instagram.

YouTube.

Reddit.

Facebook Groups.

Pinterest.

Google.

Note:

These are potential channels to validate, not guarantees.

---

# 4. THE PROBLEM YOU ARE SOLVING

Explain:

What the problem is.

Why it matters.

What frustration exists.

What people currently try.

Why existing solutions may be insufficient.

---

# 5. YOUR DIGITAL PRODUCT

## Product Name

---

## Product Format

Examples:

PDF.

Toolkit.

Template.

Checklist.

Planner.

Prompt Pack.

Mini Course.

---

## What It Could Include

Provide approximately:

3–7 components.

Example:

- Weekly planner
- Study schedule
- Focus checklist
- AI prompt pack
- Revision tracker

Do not create the entire product.

Only provide the concept.

---

# 6. THE TRANSFORMATION

Explain:

FROM:

Current situation.

↓

TO:

Desired situation.

Example:

FROM:

"I don't know where to start and constantly fall behind."

TO:

"I have a simple system for organising my study time."

---

# 7. YOUR POSITIONING

## Core Positioning Statement

Provide:

A simple sentence.

Example:

"Designed for busy students who struggle with traditional study systems."

---

# 8. YOUR MARKETING ANGLE

Provide:

One primary marketing angle.

Then:

2 alternative angles.

Example:

### Primary

"Stop trying to study like everyone else."

### Alternative

"A simpler study system for overwhelmed students."

### Alternative

"Organise your studies without complicated productivity systems."

---

# 9. RECOMMENDED BUSINESS STYLE

Recommend:

Personal Brand.

Faceless.

Hybrid.

Explain why.

---

# 10. SUGGESTED PRICE RANGE

Provide:

## ENTRY

$X–$X

## RECOMMENDED

$X–$X

## PREMIUM / BUNDLE

$X+

Explain briefly:

Why.

Do not promise:

Sales.

Income.

Conversion.

---

# 11. PRODUCT CREATION DIFFICULTY

Choose:

Easy.

Moderate.

Advanced.

Explain:

Why.

---

# 12. ESTIMATED FIRST VERSION

Explain:

What the simplest useful version could look like.

Example:

"Start with a 15–20 page toolkit rather than building a full course."

The goal:

Launch faster.

Test.

Improve.

---

# 13. CONTENT POTENTIAL

Recommend content directions.

Examples:

Problem Content.

Educational Content.

Mistakes.

Before / After.

Stories.

Tips.

Templates.

Tutorials.

Provide:

5 example content ideas.

Do not create the entire content strategy.

---

# 14. WHY THIS OPPORTUNITY MAY WORK

Explain:

Positive factors.

Examples:

Clear audience.

Specific problem.

Simple product.

Content potential.

Personal fit.

Expansion potential.

Do NOT say:

"It will work."

Say:

"This opportunity may be worth validating because..."

---

# 15. RISKS OR CHALLENGES

Be honest.

Competition.

Audience education.

Content requirements.

Trust.

Product quality.

Market validation.

---

↓

Template Bundle

↓

Course

↓

Membership

Automation

Show the possibility.

# IMPORTANT OUTPUT RESTRICTIONS

The Blueprint must NOT:

Build the complete product.

Write the complete ebook.

Create the entire sales page.

Create all social media content.

Create every marketing asset.

Create the entire launch plan.

Those belong to the paid Builder.

The Blueprint gives:

Clarity.

Direction.

Strategy.

The Builder gives:

Execution.

---

# QUALITY CHECK

Before generating a Blueprint confirm:

Is the product specific?

Is the audience clear?

Is the problem clear?

Does it fit the user?

Can the user create it?

Does the format make sense?

Does the price make sense?

Is the business style appropriate?

Is there a simple first version?

Does the user know what to do next?

If not:

Ask more questions.

Do not generate a weak Blueprint.
