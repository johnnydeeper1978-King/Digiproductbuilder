Questions

Do I need to already own the Business-in-a-Box kit?

No, but most people who book a call do — the kit explains the system, this builds and runs it for you.

What if only one or two agents actually matter for my business?

That's what the Starter Build is for. Most people start there and upgrade to the Full Roster once they see it working.

What happens after the follow-up support window ends?

The agents are yours to keep running as-is. If you want ongoing updates as your business or the underlying platforms change, that's the Managed Workforce tier.

Can I upgrade tiers later?

Yes — Starter Build credits toward a later Full Roster upgrade if you decide you need the rest of the library.
